Critique of G1

Design review only (detector doesn’t apply to a native watch layout). Judged as a glance surface: one hand wet, arm’s length, round 227 dp screen.

What works

  • One action. The orb is the only filled, bright element, so Start wins the glance.
  • Result on the bezel echoes the live Wash ring, and the Step name says what to do next.
  • History and Settings flank the orb. They stay apart, so a thumb never lands on the wrong one: keep this.

What to fix

  • P1The flanking buttons sit 5 dp from the bezel arcs, at the curved edge where touches miss. Pull them inward and give them a 48 dp hit area around a 38 dp visual.
  • P1In the Rinse build, a missed arc and a covered arc are the same blue (segCol falls through to the accent). The one piece of information the ring carries is lost.
  • P2Weight sits high: clock, name and orb crowd the top, the bottom third holds one 11 px line. Drop the orb 8 dp and stack the time under the result line so the two read together.
  • P2The orb is flat against the black; the sky-blue gradient has no lift. Add a short offset shadow and a 1 px top highlight.
  • P2Shortcut discs vanish into the black (#14263a on #000). A 1 px edge keeps them findable without making them loud.

Palette use

  • Sky blue #5bb8ff is reserved for the ring’s covered arcs and the orb, so it always means “this is what counts”.
  • Sand #f0c997 stays for borderline arcs and the all-covered line; never for missed.
  • Missed arcs use the neutral #46607a (3.3:1 on black), a gap, not an alarm. The wording rule holds: this is an estimate.
