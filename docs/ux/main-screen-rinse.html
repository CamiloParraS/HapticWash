Critique: critical issues in each

Reviewed as a watch glance surface: one hand wet, arm’s length, a round 227 dp screen. Design review only (detector doesn’t apply to a native watch layout). Spanish and the first-run state were part of the review.

Today

Hierarchy 2/4 · Information 1/4 · Fit 3/4
  • P1The ring’s “4” is a count with no referent. Nothing says which Step was missed, so the result cannot be acted on.
  • P1Two competing heroes: the bright ring and the wide gradient pill. The eye lands on the number, not on Start.
  • P2The ring hangs 5 dp under the clock pill, and the time line sits tight under the ring: the stack reads as three separate floating parts.

D · Orb

Hierarchy 3/4 · Information 3/4 · Fit 2/4
  • P1The orb is 74 dp, a smaller target than today’s 183 × 46 pill, on the primary action of a wet-hands app.
  • P1A 7 dp full ring with a 6 dp gap eats the space the orb needs, and its top sits 5 dp from the clock pill.
  • P2Caption is bold white 13 px: as loud as the orb. Icons sit 3 dp under the caption, so the lower third is a tight pile.

G1 · Thin bezel ring

Hierarchy 3/4 · Information 3/4 · Fit 3/4
  • P1History and Settings sit 5 dp from the bezel arcs, at the edge of curved glass: hard to hit, and the band orb + two icons reads as a toolbar, not a single action.
  • P1Weight is top-heavy: clock, text, orb crowd the upper half while the bottom 40 dp is empty except the time line.
  • P2Result is split in two places (step name top, time bottom) with the orb between; the eye has to bridge it.

Next: a squashed orb, and tying the arcs to Steps

Your two asks. The orb is flattened to 130 × 70 dp so the shortcuts can rise to 44 dp with 8 dp clear of the bottom arc. For the arcs: the arcs run clockwise from the top-right in the Step order (Palms, Back of hands, Between fingers, Thumbs, Fingertips), but nothing says so. Two ways to make it readable, using the Step glyphs the app already has.
