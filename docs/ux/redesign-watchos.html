HapticWash, Five Rings.
A watchOS-inspired redesign.

Every screen of the current app, rebuilt in the grammar Apple Watch made familiar: full-bleed black, an app tint washing down from the top, Activity-style concentric rings (one per Step, each in its own colour), big rounded numerals, platter lists, glass toolbar buttons along the bottom arc. Still a round 227 dp Wear OS screen, still ≥48 dp targets and estimate-only wording. Each screen shows today's capture, then English and Spanish. A checker measures every text line against the round edge in both languages.

What changes besides the look

Mechanics

  • Five concentric rings replace the five-arc ring. Each Step owns a ring and a colour, so a Step is found by position, colour and its row; status is still carried by shape (full, half, track only) and words, never colour alone.
  • Step guide pages vertically with the crown, dots on the right edge as in watchOS. Swiping right is back again, so the pull-down-to-close workaround goes away.
  • History and Settings move to glass buttons on the bottom arc, watchOS-toolbar style. Start keeps the one wide primary capsule in the middle.
  • Past the 40 s target the ring laps itself with a shadowed head, the way Activity rings show more than 100 %.
  • Twist warning becomes a watchOS-style sheet: glass ✕ and tinted ✓ on the bottom arc, so it stays a two-option choice with no new button copy.
  • The circular complication drops its label and shows the rings with a drop; the label moves to a long-text slot (new type).

Kept on purpose

  • Wear OS system pieces stay Wear OS: TimeText at the top, swipe-to-dismiss, the Tile's EdgeButton, ambient rules.
  • Hold anywhere to stop (wet hands miss buttons), the 60° gap that clears the clock, one primary action per screen.
  • Toggles take the app tint, not iOS green, and stay Wear switch-buttons in code.
